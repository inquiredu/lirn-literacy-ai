Present Kit

One deck file. A stage for the room, speaker notes in a window of their own, and a review to read again or print. No build step, nothing to install, nothing sent anywhere.

PresentThe stage. S opens the speaker notes; ? shows every key. ReviewEvery slide as a page, with the notes on request. Prints as the backup deck.

Alpha Built and checked in Chrome; not yet run in a room. The rules are in STANDARD.md, and how to start your own deck is in README.md.
